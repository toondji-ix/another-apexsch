About Semester Tech

Founded in 2024, Semester Tech is a non-profit organization dedicated to bridging the gap between education and technology. We believe that every student deserves access to high-quality learning experiences that prepare them for the future.

Our Story

Starting as a small group of passionate students at our university, we quickly grew into a community of innovators, educators, and technologists working together to create meaningful change in education.

Our Values

Innovation

Always pushing the boundaries of what's possible in education technology.

Accessibility

Making quality education available to everyone, regardless of background.

Collaboration

Working together to create solutions that benefit the entire community.

Sustainability

Building programs that have lasting impact and create sustainable change.

Excellence

Striving for the highest standards in everything we do.

Inclusion

Celebrating diversity and ensuring all voices are heard.
